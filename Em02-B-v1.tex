\section{Weighted atomic integral}
The passive \(T\)-cone is weighted by \(e^{-T}\), and
\(\int_0^\infty T e^{-T}\,\mathrm d T=1\).
The angular factor on that cone is the sphere probability.
The dynamical integral is
\begin{equation}
 I=\int \frac{S}{4\pi}\,c_\delta(S,\mathbf n)\,\mathrm d\mathbf n\,\mathrm d S,
\end{equation}
with \(c_\delta\) the translated atomic profile and \(U=U_*\).
The site is \(U_*=(1,1,0,0)\), \(V_*=0\), so \(b-a\) is future null.
Route~B is a Gauss rule on the cap containing the mollifier
support: \(24\times 16\times 24\) nodes.
Route~A is a scrambled Sobol rule of length \(2^{12}\) on the same
cap and does not call Route~B.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
profile & Gauss & Sobol\\
\midrule
\(\rho_\delta\) & \(4.851387241020615\) & \(4.845938176179326\)\\
\(\kappa_\delta\) & \(-24.16497259148322\) & \(-24.198566431394124\)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Absolute gaps are \(5.45\times 10^{-3}\) and \(3.36\times 10^{-2}\).
Relative gaps are about \(0.11\%\) and \(0.14\%\).
A coarser Gauss rule, \(12\times 8\times 12\), differs from a
\(20\times 12\times 16\) rule by \(1.5\%\) relative.
This is an independent quadrature check of a closed-form integrand.
It is not the seven-row pairing: the vertex and the component
adjoint are not in \(I\).

\section{Simulated non-tip adjoint}
With \(f=\sigma=1\) and unit scalar \(A\)-factors, the displayed
\(\mathscr P_{5a}\) reduces to
\(\partial_{s^0}^2\partial_{t^0}^2\Psi\) for
\(\Psi=\exp(-((s^0-1.5)^2+(t^0)^2))\).
Derivatives are taken before the chart evaluation.
On \(\gamma\in[-1.2,-0.4]\) and transverse radius at most \(0.8\),
with density \(\beta/(8\pi^2|\gamma|)\) at \(\beta=1.5\),
\texttt{dblquad} returns
\(-0.004633651977461527\),
error estimate \(1.6\times 10^{-15}\).
This is simulated scalar data, not the inherited component map.
